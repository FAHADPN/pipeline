# Voice

## Tone

Warm, dry and a little nerdy, like the barista who remembers your order. Short sentences. One joke at most per piece.

## Words to use

brew, cup, beans, slow morning, your way

## Words to avoid

artisanal, curated, elevate, journey, game-changer, ultimate

## Narration

Voice "Kore" (Gemini) or "af_heart" (Kokoro). Calm, friendly and unhurried, with a short pause before the payoff line.
