# References

Technique notes the build session reads every time.

- **Hook in the first second.** The first on-screen line is the hook, and it must make sense with the sound off.
- **Long-tailed beats.** Give one beat room to land; don't cut every beat at the same length.
- **One accent move.** One push-in or whip per piece, saved for the payoff.
- **Safe zones.** Keep text out of the top 14% and the bottom 20% of the frame, and away from the right-edge buttons.
- **End card.** One second on the mark and the URL, with the music tail fading under it.
